The mathematical references

Bigraded. The blue and purple curves are a visual mnemonic for the two indices of a motivic sphere. An ordinary wireframe cannot literally represent motivic weight; this is a logo, not a model of the object. Motivic spheres and P¹-stabilization ↗

Tate. The pointed projective line (P¹, ∞) represents the motivic sphere S2,1. This mark borrows the visual language of suspension; the polyhedron is an identity metaphor, not a model of a motivic space. On P¹-stabilization in unstable motivic homotopy theory ↗

Exceptional. Blowing up the affine plane at the origin replaces the point with a projective line of directions. The ellipse is a schematic stand-in for that exceptional divisor. A geometric picture of the blow-up ↗
